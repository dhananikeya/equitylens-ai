# EquityLens AI

AI-powered capital markets research platform for comparing SEC filings, financial performance, risks, and company fundamentals.

## Live Demo

[Launch EquityLens AI](https://equitylens-ai-2h7rd8qluv3bc8sdqf5cu3.streamlit.app/)

## What it does

- Compares public companies within the same industry
- Uses SEC 10-K and S-1 filing data
- Calculates revenue growth and profitability metrics
- Compares business models, platform dependencies, and risk factors
- Visualizes multi-year financial trends
- Links analysis back to source SEC filings
