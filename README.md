# EquityLens AI

EquityLens AI is a neutral public-markets research platform that turns SEC filings and structured financial data into comparable company research.

## Live Demo

[Launch EquityLens AI](https://equitylens-ai-2h7rd8qluv3bc8sdqf5cu3.streamlit.app/)

## Current Features

- Peer comparison within an industry
- Single-company research view with ticker search
- Annual 10-K financial comparison
- Latest-quarter 10-Q analysis
- Trailing-twelve-month (LTM) metrics
- "What Changed This Quarter?" factual change summaries
- Revenue and operating-margin trend charts
- Capital structure snapshots
- Risk and business-model comparison
- 10-K vs latest 10-Q filing snapshot
- Recent filing timeline with direct SEC links
- Grounded quick research questions answered only from structured filing data
- Optional private live-market-data integration
- Neutral presentation with no buy/sell recommendations, rankings, or investment picks

## Research Approach

EquityLens standardizes company-reported information so users can examine:

- Revenue growth
- Gross, operating, and net margins
- Quarterly and year-over-year changes
- LTM performance
- Cash, investments, debt, and share counts
- Business models and revenue sources
- Platform dependencies
- Competitive and operational risks
- Original SEC filing sources

**EquityLens informs. The user decides.**

## Data Use and Disclaimer

EquityLens AI is an educational and research project. It does not provide personalized investment advice, investment recommendations, rankings, or guarantees of future performance.

The public demo is designed around publicly available SEC filings and company-reported disclosures. Market-data integrations may be used in private development environments only when permitted by the relevant provider's licensing terms and exchange entitlements.

Financial information may be delayed, incomplete, or changed by later filings or restatements. Users should verify material information against original SEC filings and company disclosures before making financial decisions.
